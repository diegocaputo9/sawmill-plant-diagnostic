Process map

Name each main step in the order material flows. Open More details to add cycle time, WIP before, changeover, or notes. Everything except the name is optional. The flow chart at the bottom updates as you go.

Flow chart

Built from the steps above, in order. An orange outline marks a step flagged by the rules below.

Rules used in the analysis: a step with a cycle time at least 30% longer than the step before or after it, and the largest queue sitting in front of the slowest step. Changeover and notes are kept for context and the report. Thresholds are starting points for discussion, not standards.
